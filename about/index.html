PERSONAL BIOGRAPHY · 167 WORDS

I am Tan Yin Yun, a Bachelor of Marketing (Honours) student at Universiti Utara Malaysia, originally from Batu Pahat, Johor. Currently in Semester 7 with a CGPA of 3.93, I am interested in digital marketing, performance marketing, paid media and marketing analytics. I enjoy understanding how people make decisions and how data can help explain their behaviour.

Alongside my studies, I have worked on freelance interior visualization and gained graphic design experience at My Glory Trading. These experiences have helped me communicate with clients, respond to feedback and manage project deadlines. My university multimedia roles have also given me opportunities to contribute design work, photography and event support.

Through marketing research and content projects, I am learning to connect creative ideas with evidence. I see myself as analytical, creative and detail-oriented, while recognising that there is still much to learn. My goal is to keep improving my technical knowledge, communication and confidence, and to apply these skills during a digital marketing internship from February to June 2027.

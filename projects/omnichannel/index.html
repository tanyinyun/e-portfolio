The Rise of Omnichannel Retailing in Malaysia

A portrait infographic exploring Malaysian omnichannel retailing through industry research, market comparison and visual communication.

ROLEIndividual A3 poster
YEAR2026
TOOLSIndustry research · Infographic design
Retailing ManagementA3research infographicEDITORIAL COVER / ORIGINAL ASSETS PENDING
01 / SITUATION

Retail customers move between physical shops and digital touchpoints. The assignment examined how Malaysian retailers respond to this behaviour.

02 / TASK

Research the topic and present the most relevant evidence in a readable A3 infographic.

03 / ACTION

Combined retail industry research, competitor benchmarking and omnichannel concepts with a structured visual hierarchy.

04 / RESULT

Produced an A3 research infographic poster. The original poster and its source list are awaiting upload.

05 / REFLECTION

Condensing research into a poster taught me to prioritise information. Next time, I would make source dates more prominent and ask a reader unfamiliar with the topic to check whether the main message is clear.

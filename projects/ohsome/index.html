CASE STUDY / THE CREATIVE ANALYST

OH!SOME TikTok Store Review

An individual store-review video featuring snacks and drinks from OH!SOME, with product evaluation and engagement measurement.

ROLEIndividual project
YEAR2026
TOOLSCapCut · TikTok · Content planning
Content Marketing1,018TikTok viewsEDITORIAL COVER / ORIGINAL ASSETS PENDING
01 / SITUATION

A digital marketing assignment offered an opportunity to turn a retail visit into content that helps viewers discover products.

02 / TASK

Plan, record and edit an individual review, then evaluate the published content using available engagement metrics.

03 / ACTION

Planned the video concept, reviewed snacks and drinks, recorded the content and edited the video. Looked at views, likes, comments, saves and shares to understand audience response.

04 / RESULT

1,018 views · 49 likes · 15 comments · 9 saves · 28 shares. These are content engagement outcomes, rather than evidence of sales or paid advertising performance.

05 / REFLECTION

The project helped me connect creative decisions with audience response. A view count alone does not explain why people watched or whether they bought anything. Next time, I would test a clearer opening hook and call to action, while recording the measurement date for a fair comparison.

CASE STUDY / THE CREATIVE ANALYST

The Influence of Livestream Influencers on Gen Z Online Purchase Intention

A quantitative study of how trust, interactivity, entertainment and expertise relate to Gen Z online purchase intention.

ROLEResearch project
YEAR2025
TOOLSSPSS · Excel · Survey research
Marketing Research & Analytics150survey respondentsEDITORIAL COVER / ORIGINAL ASSETS PENDING
01 / SITUATION

Livestream shopping combines product information with entertainment and interaction. This project explored those factors among Gen Z students at public universities.

02 / TASK

Study consumer purchase intention through survey research and interpret the evidence using statistical analysis.

03 / ACTION

Worked with a sample of 150 respondents, cleaned the dataset and used SPSS for reliability testing, t-tests, ANOVA, correlation and regression analysis.

04 / RESULT

Completed a quantitative research project with 150 survey respondents. The work developed practical experience in data preparation and interpreting statistical output.

05 / REFLECTION

This experience showed me that collecting data is only one part of research. Clear variables, careful cleaning and cautious interpretation matter just as much. In future work, I would document cleaning decisions more clearly and explain the limitations of the sample alongside each conclusion.

Fun Run 2026 — Encore of Tomorrow

Multimedia work for a university running event, from participant merchandise and promotional visuals to photography and event-day support.

ROLEMultimedia Committee
YEAR2026
TOOLSVisual design · Photography · Team coordination
FUN RUN 2026 / MULTIMEDIA COMMITTEE
01 / SITUATION

A university event needed a recognisable visual identity across promotional materials, merchandise and on-site coverage.

02 / TASK

Contribute multimedia deliverables and support the team during event activities.

03 / ACTION

Worked on T-shirt, lanyard, medal and string bag designs, Facebook visuals, Instagram posters and a route map. Supported VIP and participant photography, videography, route guidance and cross-team coordination.

04 / RESULT

Delivered a range of event visuals and contributed to event-day documentation and participant support. Attendance and campaign performance are not claimed here.

05 / REFLECTION

This experience connected design work with practical event needs. It taught me to think about how visuals will be used, rather than only how they look. Next time, I would prepare a shared shot list and a clearer checklist for final asset handover.

MINI REFLECTIONS / DRAFTS FOR PERSONAL REVIEW

What experience
has taught me.

01

Analytical thinking

My marketing research project gave me experience working with survey data and interpreting SPSS output. I learned that a result needs context, and that careful preparation supports better analysis. I want to improve how I explain findings to someone without a statistics background, using concise summaries and clear visual comparisons. My next step is to practise turning analysis into a practical marketing recommendation.

02

Creative communication

Graphic design, event multimedia and interior visualization gave me different ways to communicate an idea. Each format has its own audience and practical limits. I am learning to choose information and visuals based on the message, rather than decoration alone. In future work, I want to test whether a viewer understands the main point quickly and use that feedback to refine the design.

03

Time management

Balancing freelance work with university coursework required me to consider deadlines and revisions together. I learned that a realistic schedule should include time for feedback, rather than only the first version. I want to become more consistent in breaking projects into smaller milestones. A weekly task review and a clearer record of outstanding revisions would help me manage several responsibilities more reliably.

04

Teamwork & communication

University event roles gave me opportunities to work across teams and support practical tasks beyond design. Clear communication matters when several people depend on the same information. I want to improve how I confirm responsibilities and hand over materials, especially before a busy event day. Next time, I would use a shared checklist and agree on who needs each deliverable and when.
